In this project, **Explanations** are generated at two complementary levels:

1. **Mathematical Attribution Level (SHAP Engine)** — Identifies the exact numerical features and direction pushing the probability score up or down.
2. **Natural Language Business Level (LLM / Root Cause Agent)** — Translates mathematical signals into a clear, human-readable narrative explaining *why* the customer is hesitating.

---

## 🔬 1. Mathematical Explanation (SHAP Engine)

The mathematical explanation layer is implemented in [`cart_abandonment_ml/explainability/shap_engine.py`](file:///c:/Users/Meet/Downloads/new%20flip/cart_abandonment_ml/explainability/shap_engine.py).

### How it works:
* **TreeExplainer**: The system uses `shap.TreeExplainer` on the trained XGBoost model.
* **Shapley Value Equation**: For any predicted probability $P(\text{Abandon})$, the model decomposes the score relative to the dataset baseline ($\text{Base Value} \approx 0.35$):
  $$P(\text{Abandon}) = \text{Base Value} + \sum_{i=1}^{M} \phi_i$$

* **Categorizing Factors**:
  * **Risk Drivers ($\phi_i > 0$)**: Features that push the abandonment risk higher.
  * **Retention Signals ($\phi_i < 0$)**: Features that keep the user on the site.

```python
# Output from ShapEngine.explain_session(session)
SessionExplanation(
    abandonment_probability=0.842,
    predicted_class="Likely Abandon",
    base_value=0.350,
    risk_drivers=[
        FeatureImportance(feature="total_shipping_charges", importance=+0.341, direction="increases_abandonment"),
        FeatureImportance(feature="competitor_price_checked", importance=+0.218, direction="increases_abandonment"),
        FeatureImportance(feature="hesitation_score", importance=+0.185, direction="increases_abandonment")
    ],
    retention_signals=[
        FeatureImportance(feature="is_plus_member", importance=-0.120, direction="decreases_abandonment"),
        FeatureImportance(feature="product_rating", importance=-0.085, direction="decreases_abandonment")
    ]
)
```

## 💬 2. Natural Language Business Explanation (LLM Narrative)

The mathematical SHAP values are injected directly into the **Root Cause Agent** prompt ([`cart_abandonment_ml/reasoning/root_cause_agent.py`](file:///c:/Users/Meet/Downloads/new%20flip/cart_abandonment_ml/reasoning/root_cause_agent.py)).

The LLM (Google Gemini) translates these numbers into a human-readable explanation:

```text
=== EXPLANATION GENERATED BY GEMINI ===
"The customer is exhibiting strong Price Sensitive friction. The primary reason for hesitation 
is an unexpected shipping charge (₹75.00), which contributed +34.1% to the abandonment probability. 
This was compounded by the user copy-pasting the product title to check competitor pricing 
(adding +21.8% risk). Although the user is a Flipkart Plus member (reducing risk by 12.0%), 
the shipping fee and price comparison dominated their decision to stall at checkout."
```
