## ⚙️ The 4-Step Recommendation Pipeline

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Diagnosed Root Cause + SHAP Drivers] --> B[1. ChromaDB Vector RAG Retrieval]
    B -->|Top 5 Candidate Interventions| C[2. Business Rule & Margin Auditor]
    C --> D[3. Gemini LLM Recommender Agent]
    D --> E[4. Final Actionable Intervention & Push Nudge]
```

---

### Step 1: Vector RAG Candidate Retrieval (ChromaDB)
* **Implementation**: [`cart_abandonment_ml/retrieval/rag_retriever.py`](file:///c:/Users/Meet/Downloads/new%20flip/cart_abandonment_ml/retrieval/rag_retriever.py)
* **Knowledge Base**: 200 curated e-commerce strategy documents stored in ChromaDB vector store.
* **Vector Search**: Performs semantic cosine similarity search based on the diagnosed root cause and top SHAP risk factors to retrieve the **Top 5 candidate strategies**.

---

### Step 2: Enforcing Business Rules & Margin Protection
Before choosing an intervention, the system enforces strict profit-margin guidelines:
1. **Discount Minimization Rule**: Direct cash discounts (`Medium` or `High` cost) are **prohibited** if abandonment risk is moderate ($P < 0.75$) or if the customer has a high return rate.
2. **Cost Hierarchy Preference**: Prefer zero-cost or low-cost interventions over cash discounts:
   $$\text{None / Low Cost (No-Cost EMI, Trust Badges, Free Shipping Waiver)} > \text{Medium / High Cost (Cash Discount)}$$

---

### Step 3: LLM Synthesis (Google Gemini Recommender Agent)
* **Implementation**: [`cart_abandonment_ml/recommendation/recommender_agent.py`](file:///c:/Users/Meet/Downloads/new%20flip/cart_abandonment_ml/recommendation/recommender_agent.py)
* The **Recommender Agent** prompts Gemini with the RAG candidates, shopper persona, and business cost rules to pick and adapt the single best intervention.

#### Example Prompt Data sent to Gemini:
```text
=== ANALYSIS STATE ===
Abandonment Probability : 84.2%
Root Cause              : Delivery Concern

=== CANDIDATE INTERVENTIONS FROM KNOWLEDGE BASE ===
Candidate 1: Free Shipping Waiver if order completed within 5 mins (Cost: Low, Success Rate: 82%)
Candidate 2: Express Delivery Upgrade (Cost: Low, Success Rate: 78%)
Candidate 3: Flat ₹200 Discount Voucher (Cost: Medium, Success Rate: 85%)
```

---

### Step 4: Heuristic Fallback (When Gemini is Offline)
If Gemini is unavailable, the fallback engine automatically selects the candidate from the RAG list that has the **highest empirical success rate** while meeting the cost policy.
